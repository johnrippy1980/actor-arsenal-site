Manta Scraper - Extract Business Directory Listings & Company Data

Scrape comprehensive business information from Manta.com, one of the largest US small business directories. Extract company profiles, contact details, categories, and more for lead generation and market research.

Why Use Manta Scraper?

Manta hosts millions of small business listings across every industry. This scraper enables you to:

  • Build Lead Lists: Extract verified business contact information at scale
  • Research Markets: Analyze business density by industry and location
  • Enrich CRM Data: Add company details to existing customer records
  • Verify Citations: Check NAP consistency for local SEO campaigns
  • Competitive Analysis: Research competitors in specific markets

Features

Multi-Mode Scraping

  • Search Mode: Find businesses by keyword and location
  • Profile Mode: Extract detailed data from specific company pages

Tips for Best Results

1. Be Specific: Use precise search terms for better targeting

2. Location Format: Use "City, State" format (e.g., "Austin, TX")

3. Batch by Category: Run separate searches for different industries

4. Use Proxies: Residential proxies are enabled by default for reliability
