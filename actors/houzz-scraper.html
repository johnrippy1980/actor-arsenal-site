Houzz Scraper - Extract Home Professionals, Reviews & Projects

Extract comprehensive data from Houzz including home improvement professionals, customer reviews, portfolio projects, and business details. Perfect for lead generation, market research, and competitive analysis in the home services industry.

Why Use Houzz Scraper?

Houzz is the leading platform for home remodeling and design with millions of professionals listed. This scraper helps you:

  • Generate Quality Leads: Find contractors, designers, and architects in any location
  • Analyze Competition: Research competitors' reviews, ratings, and portfolios
  • Build Contact Lists: Extract verified business contact information
  • Monitor Reviews: Track customer feedback across multiple professionals
  • Market Research: Understand pricing and service offerings in specific markets

Input Configuration

Basic Search

{

"scrapeType": "search",


"searchTerm": "General Contractor",


"location": "Los Angeles, CA",


"maxResults": 100


}

Profile Scraping

{

"scrapeType": "professional_profile",


"professionalUrl": "https://www.houzz.com/professionals/general-contractor/abc-construction",


"includeReviews": true,


"maxReviewsPerProfessional": 50


}

Advanced Options

{

"scrapeType": "search",


"searchTerm": "Kitchen Remodeler",


"location": "Miami, FL",

Tips for Best Results

1. Use Specific Locations: City + State format works best (e.g., "Denver, CO")

2. Filter by Rating: Set minRating to focus on quality professionals

3. Include Reviews: Customer reviews provide valuable insights

4. Batch by Area: Split large geographic areas into smaller searches

Frequently Asked Questions

How current is the data?

Data is scraped in real-time directly from Houzz.

Can I scrape multiple locations?

Yes, run separate scrapes for each location or use the Apify scheduler for automation.

What about blocked requests?

The scraper uses residential proxies by default for reliable access.

Is there a limit on results?

Maximum 500 professionals per run. For larger datasets, split into multiple runs.
