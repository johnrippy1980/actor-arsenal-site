Features

  • Ad creative extraction - Images, videos, and carousels
  • Copy analysis - Headlines, descriptions, CTAs
  • Platform targeting - Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, Audience Network
  • Demographic insights - Age, gender, location targeting
  • Spend estimates - Impression ranges and estimated spend
  • Status filtering - Active vs. inactive ads

Use Cases

  • Social ad research - See what's working on Facebook/Instagram
  • Creative inspiration - Find winning ad formats and copy
  • Competitor monitoring - Track competitor ad activity
  • Industry benchmarks - Understand advertising trends
  • Client reporting - Document competitor ad strategies
