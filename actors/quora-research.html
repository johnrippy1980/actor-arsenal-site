Research trending questions and expert answers on Quora for content marketing insights. Discover what your audience is asking, identify content gaps, and find authoritative answers to inform your content strategy.

Features

  • Question Discovery: Find relevant questions in your niche
  • Answer Analysis: Extract top answers with upvote counts
  • Topic Tracking: Identify trending topics and themes
  • Expert Insights: Find industry experts and thought leaders
  • Content Ideas: Generate blog post and FAQ ideas from real questions

Use Cases

  • Content Marketing: Find topics your audience cares about
  • SEO Research: Discover long-tail keyword opportunities
  • Thought Leadership: Identify questions to answer for visibility
  • Competitor Research: See what experts are saying in your space
  • FAQ Generation: Build FAQs from real user questions

Input Configuration

{

"demoMode": false,


"searchQuery": "local SEO strategies",


"topics": ["SEO", "Digital Marketing"],


"maxQuestions": 50,


"includeAnswers": true,


"minAnswerUpvotes": 10,


"sortBy": "relevance"


}

Content Research Tips

1. Find Content Gaps: Questions with many followers but few answers = opportunity

2. Identify Experts: Authors with high credentials are potential influencers

3. Track Trends: Sort by recent to see emerging topics

4. Long-form Ideas: High-upvote answers often make great blog post outlines

5. FAQ Building: Multiple similar questions = FAQ section material
