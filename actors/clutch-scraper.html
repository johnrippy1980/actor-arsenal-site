---

Scrape B2B agency profiles from Clutch.co. Extract company info, reviews, ratings, pricing, services, clients, and portfolios. Perfect for B2B lead generation, competitive analysis, and vendor research.

Features

  • Agency Profiles: Name, location, website, phone, description
  • Ratings & Reviews: Star ratings, review counts, detailed reviews
  • Pricing Info: Minimum project size, hourly rates
  • Services: Full list of services offered
  • Client Lists: Notable clients and portfolio
  • Filters: Search by category, location, minimum rating

Use Cases

  • B2B Lead Generation: Find agencies to partner with or sell to
  • Competitive Analysis: Research competitors in your space
  • Vendor Selection: Find the right agency for your project
  • Market Research: Understand agency landscape in any vertical

Input Configuration

{

"demoMode": false,


"searchQuery": "digital marketing agency",


"location": "New York",


"category": "digital-marketing",


"minRating": 4.5,


"maxResults": 50,


"includeReviews": true


}
