Scrape G2.com for B2B software product reviews, ratings, comparisons, and competitive intelligence. Essential for SaaS marketing and software market research.

Why G2 Data is Valuable

G2 is the leading B2B software review platform:

  • User Reviews: Verified reviews from actual software users
  • G2 Ratings: Industry-standard satisfaction scores
  • Market Position: G2 Grid placement and category rankings
  • Competitive Intelligence: Alternative products and comparisons
  • Buyer Intent Data: Used by millions to make purchase decisions
  • Enterprise Focus: Segmented reviews by company size

Features

  • Product Profile Scraping: Complete software product profiles
  • Category Search: Find top products in any G2 category
  • Review Extraction: User reviews with ratings and sentiment
  • Alternative Products: Competitor analysis data
  • Pricing Information: Available pricing tiers
  • G2 Grid Position: Leader, High Performer, Contender, Niche

Use Cases

  • Competitive Intelligence: Track competitor ratings and reviews
  • Market Research: Analyze software category trends
  • Product Marketing: Monitor your G2 presence
  • Lead Generation: Identify companies using competitor software
  • Sales Enablement: Build competitor battle cards
  • Investment Research: Evaluate SaaS companies

Example Usage

Search for CRM Software

{

"scrapeType": "category_search",


"category": "CRM Software",


"minRating": 4.0,


"companySize": "mid_market",


"maxResults": 50


}

Scrape Single Product Profile

{

"scrapeType": "product_profile",


"productUrl": "https://www.g2.com/products/hubspot-crm/reviews",


"includeReviews": true,


"maxReviewsPerProduct": 50


}

Find Salesforce Alternatives

{

"scrapeType": "alternatives",


"productName": "Salesforce",


"maxResults": 20


}

Pricing

This actor uses pay-per-event pricing:

Example costs:
  • 50 products with 25 reviews each: $0.10 + (50 × $0.08) + (1250 × $0.005) = $10.35
  • 100 products, no reviews: $0.10 + (100 × $0.08) = $8.10

Rate Limiting & Best Practices

  • Built-in delays to respect G2 servers
  • Residential proxy recommended for reliability
  • Maximum 500 products per run
  • Reviews limited per product to prevent excessive requests

Legal Considerations

  • Use data for legitimate business research purposes
  • Comply with G2 Terms of Service
  • Do not scrape personal reviewer information
  • Respect rate limits and access patterns
