Scrape Better Business Bureau (BBB) business profiles, ratings, complaints, reviews, and accreditation data. Get comprehensive trust signals and reputation data for any business.

Why BBB Data is Valuable

The BBB is one of the most trusted sources for business reputation:

  • Official Ratings: Letter grades (A+ to F) based on complaint history
  • Accreditation Status: BBB accredited vs non-accredited businesses
  • Complaint History: Detailed complaint counts, types, and resolutions
  • Customer Reviews: Verified customer feedback and ratings
  • Business Details: Years in business, contact info, license verification
  • Trust Score: Comprehensive trustworthiness assessment

Features

  • Profile Scraping: Get complete BBB profile data for any business
  • Search: Find businesses by name, location, and category
  • Complaint Data: Extract complaint counts, types, and resolution status
  • Review Extraction: Scrape customer reviews with ratings
  • Accreditation Check: Verify BBB accreditation status
  • Bulk Processing: Scrape multiple businesses in one run

Use Cases

  • Due Diligence: Verify business legitimacy before partnerships
  • Competitor Research: Compare BBB ratings with competitors
  • Lead Qualification: Filter leads by BBB rating and accreditation
  • Reputation Monitoring: Track your BBB profile and complaints
  • Market Research: Analyze industry complaint patterns
  • Citation Building: Verify NAP data from authoritative source

Example Usage

Scrape Single Business Profile

{

"scrapeType": "business_profile",


"businessUrl": "https://www.bbb.org/us/ca/los-angeles/profile/plumber/abc-plumbing-0123-456789"


}

Search for Businesses

{

"scrapeType": "search",


"businessName": "plumbing",


"location": "Los Angeles, CA",


"accreditedOnly": true,


"minRating": "A",


"maxResults": 25


}

Get Complaints Only

{

"scrapeType": "complaints",


"businessUrl": "https://www.bbb.org/us/ca/los-angeles/profile/...",


"includeComplaints": true,


"includeReviews": false


}

Important Notes

Rate Limiting

  • Built-in delays to respect BBB servers
  • Residential proxy recommended for large runs
  • Maximum 500 businesses per run

Data Accuracy

  • Ratings updated by BBB regularly
  • Complaint data is from last 3 years
  • Some businesses may have incomplete profiles

Legal Considerations

  • Use data for legitimate business purposes
  • Comply with BBB Terms of Service
  • Do not use for spam or harassment
