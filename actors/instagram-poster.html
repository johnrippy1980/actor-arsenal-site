Automate posting images to Instagram! Supports captions and hashtags.

Features

  • Image Posts - Post photos from URL
  • Captions - Add descriptions to your posts
  • Hashtags - Include relevant hashtags
  • Location Tags - Add location (when available)

Authentication

Cookies are strongly recommended - Instagram has aggressive bot detection.

1. Cookies - Export cookies from logged-in session (recommended)

2. Username/Password - May trigger verification

Example Input

Basic Post

{

"cookies": "[{...exported cookies...}]",


"imageUrl": "https://example.com/product-photo.jpg",


"caption": "Introducing our new spring collection! Fresh styles for the new season.",


"hashtags": ["fashion", "springcollection", "newstyles", "shoplocal"]


}

Business Post

{

"cookies": "[{...}]",


"imageUrl": "https://example.com/team-photo.jpg",


"caption": "Meet our amazing team! We're here to help you with all your needs. Stop by and say hello!",


"hashtags": ["teamwork", "smallbusiness", "supportlocal", "behindthescenes"]


}

Output

{

"success": true,


"postId": "ABC123...",


"postUrl": "https://instagram.com/p/ABC123..."


}

Tips

  • Use high-quality square or 4:5 ratio images
  • Keep captions engaging and authentic
  • Use 5-15 relevant hashtags
  • Post during peak engagement hours

Important Notes

  • Instagram has strong bot detection - always use cookies
  • First-time posts may require phone verification
  • Avoid posting too frequently (space posts by at least 1 hour)
