Scrape startup and company data from Crunchbase. Extract funding information, investors, founders, and company details. Perfect for market research, lead generation, and competitive intelligence.

Features

  • Company Profiles: Name, description, website, headquarters, founded date
  • Funding Data: Total funding, rounds, amounts, investors
  • Team Info: Founders and key executives
  • Industry Tags: Categories and sectors
  • Social Links: LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook
  • Filters: Industry, location, funding stage, minimum funding

Use Cases

  • Market Research: Discover competitors and market trends
  • Lead Generation: Find funded companies to sell to
  • Investment Research: Track funding activity in sectors
  • Competitive Intelligence: Monitor competitor funding and growth
  • Sales Prospecting: Build lists of well-funded startups

Input Configuration

{

"demoMode": false,


"searchQuery": "AI startup",


"category": "artificial-intelligence",


"location": "San Francisco",


"fundingStage": "series-a",


"minFunding": "$1M",


"maxResults": 50,


"includeFundingRounds": true


}
