Scrape IT service provider profiles from GoodFirms.co. Extract company info, reviews, ratings, services, and client portfolios. Perfect for B2B vendor research and competitive analysis.

Features

  • Company Profiles: Name, location, website, phone, description
  • Ratings & Reviews: Star ratings, review counts, detailed reviews
  • Pricing Info: Minimum project size, hourly rates
  • Services: Full list of services offered
  • Client Lists: Notable clients and portfolio
  • Filters: Search by category, location, minimum rating

Use Cases

  • Vendor Research: Find the right IT partner for your project
  • B2B Lead Generation: Build lists of potential clients or partners
  • Competitive Analysis: Research competitors in your industry
  • Market Research: Understand the service provider landscape

Input Configuration

{

"demoMode": false,


"searchQuery": "web development company",


"location": "New York",


"category": "web-development-companies",


"minRating": 4.5,


"maxResults": 50,


"includeReviews": true


}
