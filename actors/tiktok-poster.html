Automate posting videos to TikTok! Upload videos with captions, hashtags, and privacy settings.

Features

  • Video Upload - Post videos from URL
  • Captions & Hashtags - Add descriptions and tags
  • Privacy Controls - Set visibility, comments, duet, stitch
  • Auto-download - Automatically fetches video from URL

Authentication

Cookies are strongly recommended - TikTok has aggressive bot detection.

1. Cookies - Export cookies from logged-in session (recommended)

2. Username/Password - May trigger verification

Exporting Cookies

1. Install a cookie export extension (e.g., "EditThisCookie")

2. Log into TikTok in your browser

3. Export cookies as JSON

4. Paste the JSON in the cookies field

Example Input

Basic Video

{

"cookies": "[{...exported cookies...}]",


"videoUrl": "https://example.com/my-video.mp4",


"caption": "Check out this amazing local business!",


"hashtags": ["localbusiness", "smallbusiness", "supportlocal"]


}

Video with Privacy Settings

{

"cookies": "[{...}]",


"videoUrl": "https://example.com/video.mp4",


"caption": "Behind the scenes look at our process",


"hashtags": ["behindthescenes", "business"],


"allowComments": true,


"allowDuet": false,


"allowStitch": false,


"isPrivate": false


}

Private Video (Draft)

{

"cookies": "[{...}]",


"videoUrl": "https://example.com/draft-video.mp4",


"caption": "Testing this video before publishing",


"isPrivate": true


}

Output

{

"success": true,


"videoUrl": "https://tiktok.com/@username/video/..."


}

Tips

  • Use MP4 format for best compatibility
  • Keep videos under 10 minutes (3 min ideal)
  • Vertical videos (9:16) perform best
  • Add 3-5 relevant hashtags
  • Use trending sounds when possible

Important Notes

  • TikTok has strong bot detection - cookies are essential
  • First-time uploads may require phone verification
  • Video processing may take a few minutes after upload
