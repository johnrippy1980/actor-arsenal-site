Scrape TripAdvisor business listings, traveler reviews, bubble ratings, and photos. Perfect for hotels, restaurants, attractions, and vacation rentals.

Why TripAdvisor Data is Valuable

TripAdvisor is the world's largest travel platform:

  • Bubble Ratings: 1-5 bubble ratings from millions of travelers
  • Review Volume: Extensive traveler reviews with trip details
  • Global Coverage: Businesses in every country and city
  • Rich Details: Amenities, rankings, price levels, photos
  • Verified Reviews: Authentic traveler experiences

Features

  • Profile Scraping: Get complete business profile data
  • Search: Find businesses by name, type, and location
  • Review Extraction: Scrape traveler reviews with ratings and trip types
  • Photo URLs: Extract traveler and business photos
  • Multi-Category: Hotels, restaurants, attractions, vacation rentals
  • Bulk Processing: Scrape multiple listings efficiently

Use Cases

  • Competitive Analysis: Compare ratings across properties
  • Lead Generation: Find hotels/restaurants in specific destinations
  • Reputation Monitoring: Track your TripAdvisor presence
  • Market Research: Analyze travel industry trends
  • Review Aggregation: Combine TripAdvisor data with other platforms
  • Pricing Intelligence: Track competitor pricing

Example Usage

Scrape Single Listing

{

"scrapeType": "business_profile",


"businessUrl": "https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g123-d456789-Reviews-Example_Hotel.html",


"includeReviews": true,


"maxReviewsPerBusiness": 50


}

Search for Hotels

{

"scrapeType": "search",


"searchTerm": "beach resort",


"location": "Miami Beach, FL",


"businessType": "hotels",


"minRating": 4.0,


"maxResults": 100


}

Search for Restaurants

{

"scrapeType": "search",


"searchTerm": "Italian restaurant",


"location": "New York, NY",


"businessType": "restaurants",


"minRating": 4.5,


"maxResults": 50


}

Pricing

This actor uses pay-per-event pricing:

Example costs:
  • 50 hotels with 20 reviews each: $0.10 + (50 × $0.03) + (1000 × $0.003) = $4.60
  • 100 restaurants, no reviews: $0.10 + (100 × $0.03) = $3.10

Rate Limiting & Best Practices

  • Built-in delays to respect TripAdvisor servers
  • Residential proxy required for reliability
  • Maximum 500 listings per run
  • Reviews limited to prevent excessive requests

Data Accuracy Notes

  • Ratings are real-time bubble ratings
  • Review counts may change frequently
  • Rankings are location-specific
  • Some listings have limited public data

Legal Considerations

  • Use data for legitimate business purposes
  • Comply with TripAdvisor Terms of Service
  • Do not use for spam or fake reviews
  • Respect robots.txt guidelines
