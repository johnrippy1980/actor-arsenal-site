Scrape Angi (formerly Angie's List) business listings, customer reviews, ratings, and project photos. Essential for home service businesses and contractors.

Why Angi Data is Valuable

Angi is the leading home services marketplace:

  • Verified Reviews: Real customer reviews with project details
  • Service Professionals: Licensed, insured contractors
  • Project Photos: Before/after portfolio images
  • Service Areas: Geographic coverage information
  • Pricing Insights: Cost estimates and quotes

Features

  • Profile Scraping: Complete business profile data
  • Search: Find contractors by service type and location
  • Review Extraction: Customer reviews with ratings and projects
  • Photo Extraction: Project and portfolio photos
  • Service Categories: Plumbers, electricians, HVAC, remodelers, etc.
  • Bulk Processing: Scrape multiple listings efficiently

Use Cases

  • Competitive Analysis: Compare contractor ratings
  • Lead Generation: Find service professionals in target areas
  • Reputation Monitoring: Track your Angi presence
  • Market Research: Analyze home service industry trends
  • Pricing Intelligence: Understand market rates
  • Review Aggregation: Combine with other platforms

Example Usage

Scrape Single Business

{

"scrapeType": "business_profile",


"businessUrl": "https://www.angi.com/companylist/us/fl/miami/example-plumber.htm",


"includeReviews": true,


"maxReviewsPerBusiness": 50


}

Search for Plumbers

{

"scrapeType": "search",


"searchTerm": "plumber",


"location": "Miami, FL",


"serviceType": "plumbing",


"minRating": 4.0,


"maxResults": 100


}

Search for Electricians

{

"scrapeType": "search",


"searchTerm": "electrician",


"location": "Los Angeles, CA",


"serviceType": "electrical",


"minRating": 4.5,


"maxResults": 50


}

Pricing

This actor uses pay-per-event pricing:

Example costs:
  • 50 contractors with 20 reviews each: $0.10 + (50 × $0.04) + (1000 × $0.004) = $6.10
  • 100 contractors, no reviews: $0.10 + (100 × $0.04) = $4.10

Rate Limiting & Best Practices

  • Built-in delays to respect Angi servers
  • Residential proxy required for reliability
  • Maximum 500 listings per run
  • Reviews limited to prevent excessive requests
