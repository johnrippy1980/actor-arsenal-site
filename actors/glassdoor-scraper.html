Scrape Glassdoor for company reviews, employee salaries, interview experiences, CEO approval ratings, and comprehensive employer data. Essential for HR research, employer branding, and competitive intelligence.

Why Glassdoor Data is Valuable

Glassdoor is the leading employer review platform:

  • Employee Reviews: Authentic reviews from current and former employees
  • Salary Data: Real salary reports by job title and location
  • Interview Experiences: Detailed interview process descriptions
  • CEO Approval: Leadership approval ratings
  • Company Culture: Work-life balance, benefits, career opportunities
  • Industry Benchmarks: Compare employers within industries

Features

  • Company Profile Scraping: Complete employer profiles with ratings
  • Employee Reviews: Pros, cons, advice to management
  • Salary Information: Base pay, bonuses, total compensation by role
  • Interview Experiences: Questions, difficulty, outcome
  • Benefits Analysis: Health, retirement, perks ratings
  • CEO Approval: Leadership sentiment over time
  • Company Comparisons: Side-by-side employer analysis

Use Cases

  • HR & Recruiting: Understand employer brand perception
  • Competitive Intelligence: Monitor competitor employee sentiment
  • Job Seekers: Research potential employers
  • Salary Benchmarking: Compare compensation across companies
  • Culture Analysis: Assess company culture fit
  • Talent Acquisition: Identify recruitment opportunities

Example Usage

Search for Tech Companies

{

"scrapeType": "company_profile",


"industry": "Technology",


"minRating": 4.0,


"companySize": "1001-5000",


"maxResults": 50


}

Scrape Single Company Profile

{

"scrapeType": "company_profile",


"companyUrl": "https://www.glassdoor.com/Overview/Working-at-Google-EI_IE9079.htm",


"includeReviews": true,


"includeSalaries": true,


"maxReviewsPerCompany": 50


}

Compare Companies

{

"scrapeType": "compare",


"companyName": "Google",


"industry": "Technology",


"maxResults": 10


}

Get Interview Experiences

{

"scrapeType": "interviews",


"companyName": "Amazon",


"maxResults": 100


}

Pricing

This actor uses pay-per-event pricing:

Example costs:
  • 50 companies with 25 reviews + salaries: $0.10 + (50 × $0.08) + (1250 × $0.005) + (1250 × $0.003) = $14.10
  • 100 companies, no reviews: $0.10 + (100 × $0.08) = $8.10

Rate Limiting & Best Practices

  • Built-in delays to respect Glassdoor servers
  • Residential proxy required for reliable access
  • Maximum 200 companies per run recommended
  • Reviews limited per company to prevent excessive requests

Legal Considerations

  • Use data for legitimate business research purposes
  • Comply with Glassdoor Terms of Service
  • Do not scrape personal employee information
  • Respect rate limits and access patterns
