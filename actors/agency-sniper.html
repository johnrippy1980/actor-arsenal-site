Agency Sniper - Find Marketing Agency Clients

REVERSE PROSPECTING

Discover who marketing agencies work with using reverse prospecting. Find proven buyers by analyzing agency portfolios, case studies, client pages, footer attributions, social media mentions, and Google reviews. 6-layer discovery engine with AI-powered extraction.

Run on Apify View API
Stop cold prospecting. Start smart prospecting. If a company already hired a marketing agency, they're a proven buyer. Agency Sniper finds these pre-qualified leads for you.

Why Agency Sniper?

Traditional B2B prospecting is broken. Cold lists are garbage with 90% of "leads" having no budget or intent. Intent data from Bombora, G2, and ZoomInfo costs $20K+/year. SDRs waste time calling companies that will never buy.

Reverse prospecting flips the script:

  • Proven Buyers: If a company hired Agency X, they have budget for marketing services
  • Known Investors: These companies already understand the value of professional services
  • Active Spenders: They're not "thinking about it" - they're actively paying for marketing
  • Competitor Intel: See exactly who your competitors' agencies serve

6-Layer Discovery Engine

Agency Sniper uses a sophisticated multi-layer approach to find agency clients:

Layer 1: Website Crawling

Automatically scans 12 common client-related URL patterns: /clients, /portfolio, /work, /case-studies, /testimonials, and more. Uses Firecrawl for reliable JavaScript-rendered scraping.

Layer 2: Google Search

Searches Google for agency mentions, press releases, award announcements, and third-party testimonials using 5 query patterns.

Layer 3: AI Extraction

Uses Claude AI (Anthropic) to intelligently extract company names from unstructured content, assign confidence scores, and classify industries.

Layer 4: Footer Attribution

Finds "Powered by", "Built by", "Website by", "Designed by" credits linking to the agency. High-confidence proof of client relationship.

Layer 5: Social Media Scanning

Searches YouTube, Vimeo, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, and Twitter for agency client mentions and project announcements.

Layer 6: Google Reviews

Identifies clients through agency Google Business Profile reviews from business owners who thank the agency.

FAQ

Q: How accurate is client discovery?
A: Case studies are 95-100% accurate, portfolio pages are 85-95% accurate, and Google search is 70-85% accurate. Focus on confidence scores >= 70 for outreach.

Q: Do you find ALL of an agency's clients?
A: No. We only find publicly mentioned clients. Many agencies don't disclose all clients due to NDAs or client preference. Expect to find 20-50% of actual clients.

Q: Can I use this for any type of agency?
A: Yes! Works for digital marketing, SEO, PPC, web design, PR, branding, and creative agencies.

Q: Is this legal?
A: Yes. Agency Sniper only reads publicly available information. There's no login or authentication - just web scraping of public pages.
