11 Scrapers Upgraded with Camoufox Stealth Browsing

Bypassing Cloudflare and bot detection just got a whole lot easier

The Problem

If you've been scraping websites lately, you know the drill: Cloudflare challenges, CAPTCHAs, and bot detection have made life hell for legitimate data extraction. Many of our scrapers were hitting walls that standard browser fingerprinting just couldn't get around.

The Solution: Camoufox

Camoufox is a stealth-modified Firefox browser that handles fingerprint spoofing at the C++ level - not through JavaScript injection that can be detected. Combined with Crawlee's built-in handleCloudflareChallenge() helper, these scrapers can now slip past most bot detection systems.

What's Different?

Unlike traditional fingerprint injection libraries, Camoufox modifies the browser binary itself. This means detection scripts can't find the telltale signs of JavaScript-based fingerprint spoofing.
